# Executive Summary — Workforce Attrition Risk Analysis at Palo Alto Networks

## What This Means
Our analysis reveals critical patterns in employee turnover at Palo Alto Networks. By understanding the key drivers of attrition—specifically around workload, tenure, and department—we can transition from reactive replacements to proactive retention strategies. The data clearly shows that targeted interventions in scheduling, onboarding, and departmental support could significantly reduce turnover and its associated costs.

## Key Findings
- **High Overall Attrition:** Out of 1470 employees analyzed, 390 have exited, resulting in a 26.5% attrition rate.
- **The "Early-Tenure" Danger Zone:** Early-tenure attrition is at 33.3%, meaning employees with 2 years or less at the company are leaving at much higher rates than their more tenured peers.
- **Workload is a Major Driver:** Our Workload Attrition Index is 1.95x, indicating that employees who work overtime and travel frequently leave nearly twice as fast. Specifically, overtime alone drives a 46.3% attrition rate compared to 18.9% for non-overtime workers. Combining overtime with frequent travel pushes the attrition rate to a staggering 51.7%.
- **Demographic Variances:** Marital status plays a role, with single employees experiencing 33.1% attrition, compared to 25.1% for married and 19.5% for divorced employees.
- **Departmental Hotspots:** The HR department sees the highest attrition at 36.5%, followed by Sales at 30.8%, and R&D at 24.1%.

## Recommended Actions
1. **Redesign Workloads and Overtime:** Implement stricter controls and equitable distribution for overtime and travel, especially for roles currently experiencing the 51.7% combined attrition rate.
2. **Revamp Early-Tenure Support:** Develop a robust mentorship and check-in program specifically targeting employees in their first two years to reduce the 33.3% early-tenure attrition.
3. **Targeted Departmental Interventions:** Conduct "stay interviews" and immediate reviews within the HR and Sales departments to identify and address the root causes of their exceptionally high turnover.
4. **Flexible Benefits for Diverse Demographics:** Enhance support systems and flexible working arrangements tailored to the needs of single employees to improve their retention.
5. **Continuous Monitoring:** Deploy the interactive dashboard to management to track these specific risk factors in real-time, allowing for proactive, rather than reactive, management of at-risk talent.
